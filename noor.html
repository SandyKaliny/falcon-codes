What is
Noor City
Noor City is a mix-use development aiming to meet demand in East Cairo and located in front of the New Administrative Capital (NAC). The project will be implemented on an area of 5,000 acres Noor is designed based on the world's latest trends for integrated cities, creating a paradigm shift in the urban development map in Egypt and shall accommodate about 600,000 residents. it is aimed to be a green smart city that is meeting 21st century 4G cities standards and will include a set of integrated services and modern applications by using the latest technologies. The project will utilize the latest technologies as smart irrigation methods to decrease water consumption, the use of clean energy, smart environment friendly transportation, as well as smart lighting system for the whole city. Noor City contains various residential and commercial facilities such as residential buildings and villas, Grand Mosques, Church, Grand Malls, University, Schools and Sporting Club.
