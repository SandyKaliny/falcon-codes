Overview
NOBU Hotels blend modern cool luxury and minimal Japanese tradition into the concept of a lifestyle hotel. NOBU hotel and restaurant interact together to provide NOBU products and services that share the common characteristics of unique NOBU food experiences. Each provides a local experience with a modern design and stunning spaces. Helmed from world-renowned Chef Nobu Matsuhisa, Robert De Niro, and Meir Teper, the NOBU hotel concept is a curated experience where every property is different.
Project Description:
The EDNC project is a regeneration project located at New Cairo on 90 Street, adjacent to the AUC. The restaurant and hotel will occupy two existing buildings within the EDNC retail area. The building designated for the hotel was originally designed as a theatre, necessitating significant construction and structural modifications to transform it into a hotel with a total of 50 keys. On the contrary, the restaurant building with total area 2,124 m2 will not need any major changes. Both buildings will be connected on the ground level, sharing the same BOH
