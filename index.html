Who We Are
Atrium Project Management (APM) was founded to spearhead mega projects, beginning with "Noor"—the visionary new city in Egypt's New Administrative Capital—alongside several other major developments in Eastern Egypt. APM is dedicated to delivering exceptional project management outcomes by leveraging cutting-edge technology and the expertise of our highly qualified teams. At APM, we introduce the innovative concept of "Risk Sharing," fostering a collaborative partnership between the project owner and the project manager. By taking on shared responsibility, providing real-time information, and steering projects forward with precision, we ensure that costs remain within targeted levels while achieving outstanding results.

Our Story

Atrium Project Management (APM) was founded to redefine project excellence in Egypt and the Region. Emerging from the legacy of Talaat Moustafa Group, APM led the management of Noor City in the New Administrative Capital and continued to oversee major developments across Egypt. With our unique "Risk Sharing" Model, we build trusted partnerships that drive innovation, transparency, and sustainable success.
