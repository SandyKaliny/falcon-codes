SOUTHMED
PROJECT

Project Summary:
South MED is one of Egypt’s most ambitious coastal developments, conceived as a world-class, fully integrated waterfront destination on the Southern Mediterranean. Developed by Talaat Moustafa Group (TMG), the project represents a landmark investment in Egypt’s tourism, real estate, and infrastructure sectors, redefining the scale and quality of mixed-use resort developments in the region. Strategically positioned at Kilo 165 on the Alexandria–Matruh Road, South MED enjoys direct access to the Mediterranean shoreline while remaining highly accessible, with Al Alamein International Airport located just 18 minutes away. This prime location places the development within a 3–4-hour flight radius of major European and Gulf cities, positioning it to attract international tourism, second-home buyers, and long-stay visitors. Designed to global standards by internationally recognized master planners, South MED is envisioned as a destination that balances luxury, accessibility, sustainability, and long-term economic value, reinforcing Egypt’s role as a year-round Mediterranean destination.
Overview
South MED is a landmark, multi-phase coastal development spanning approximately 23 million square meters, combining residential, hospitality, leisure, and lifestyle destinations within a fully integrated master plan. Communities are seamlessly connected to amenities, open spaces, and a comprehensive infrastructure and public realm network. The development features a world-class marina and waterfront, activated beachfronts, retail and dining districts, hotels and resort-style residences, sports and wellness facilities, golf courses, Crystal swimmable lagoons, and public spaces that promote placemaking and social engagement. Designed with a contemporary architectural vision and sensitivity to the coastal environment, South MED is set to generate long-term economic value through job creation and tourism growth, reinforcing its role in Egypt’s future coastal development. As one of the Project Managers for TMG, we APM, contribute to safeguarding the project vision and ensure its delivery through coordinated planning, design management, procurement, cost and schedule control, construction governance, and proactive risk and quality management across all phases.
