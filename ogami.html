Overview
(Nobu North Coast in Egypt, located at the heart of one of the fastest-growing luxury destinations in middle east region, Ras El Hekma area, including: Nobu Hotel, Restaurant and luxuries Residences. Served by Lobby area, lounge, Fitness & spa, Adult & kids pool, Rooftop lounge & bar and beach club.)
Project Description:
NOBU OGAMI Development is SODIC newest project located on the North Coast of Egypt. The site is determined by a 780 meters beach front on one of the most beautiful beaches on the coast. The project area (37 Acres) lies within a developed Master Plan for 440 Acres, and the project components are branded villas, residential units, retails facilities, 5-stars hotel, and basic amenities. All of the hospitality and residence components shall be operated by the same hotel operator.
