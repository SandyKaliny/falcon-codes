ABOUT US

At a Glance

Atrium Project Management (APM) as an established Project Management Firm aims for partnership with Industry Leaders to achieve the needed results out of the development of their projects. With well experienced staff and top-notch technology solutions; APM derives its spirit to be the winning horse in project achievement. Our journey covers prestigious projects in the countries of Arab Republic of Egypt like "Noor" City and MENA Region like Banan Project-Riyadh. The company focuses on delivering top-quality project management using advanced technology and expert teams.

APM also introduces a unique "Risk Sharing" concept, creating a partnership between project owners and managers to share responsibility, maintain cost control, achieve excellent results, and securing every stakeholder’s satisfaction.

What We Do Best

Our Services

Construction Management

Comprehensive oversight of all construction activities in coordination with the supervision consultant, ensuring best practices are followed and full adherence to project specifications and requirements.

Procurement & Tender Management

Implementation of advanced tendering strategies and high-quality documentation to select the most qualified consultants and contractors. Includes the ability to adopt various procurement models such as Design–Bid–Build, Design–Build, and others.

Projects Controls

Holistic oversight and direction of project control systems throughout the project life cycle, including performance tracking, progress evaluation, and recommending corrective actions across all disciplines.

Time Management

Planning, scheduling, and managing all time-related aspects of the project, ensuring proper coordination among stakeholders to achieve timely project delivery.

Cost Management & Value Engineering

Continuous monitoring of project costs from inception through completion, supported by detailed analysis to identify alternative solutions and cost-saving opportunities while preserving quality and functionality.

Design Management

Proactive management of the design process across all stages, ensuring client requirements are met, integrating the latest technologies, and maintaining compliance with all applicable codes and standards.
