About Us

At a Glance

Atrium Project Management (APM) leads major projects like the "Noor" city in Egypt's New Administrative Capital. The company focuses on delivering top-quality project management using advanced technology and expert teams.

APM also introduces a unique "Risk Sharing" concept, creating a partnership between project owners and managers to share responsibility, maintain cost control, and achieve excellent results.
